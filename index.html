02 THE PERSON BEHIND THE ART

Hi, I’m Amy.
aka Esshuttr.

Photographer, illustrator and music person — drawn to the split seconds where everything comes alive.

I’m a London-based image-maker looking to uplift the alternative music scene. I first picked up a camera to photograph a friend’s band in January 2024 — with no experience and no camera of my own. One show changed everything.

Now I’m documenting the artists, crowds and places that make this scene feel like home, while exploring how illustration and moving image can take the story further.

“I never knew how much picking up a camera could change my life.”
